FAQs

Questions students and parents ask.

Do you teach all subjects?

We support a broad range of school and examination subjects. Contact us with the subject, level and exam board so we can match you with the right support.

Which international exam boards do you support?

We support Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel, AQA and OCR, with lessons tailored to the relevant specification.

Are lessons fully online?

Yes. Online lessons make it possible to learn from wherever you are using a phone, tablet or laptop.

Can I request exam-focused revision?

Yes. Tell us your exam board, subject, level and areas you want to improve, and we can plan focused support.
